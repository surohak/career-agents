# Voice

- Person: first person on LinkedIn and the site ("I build..."), third person only in press-style bios.
- Sentences: short, concrete, one idea each. Lead with the outcome, then the how.
- Punctuation: no em dashes, use a comma or a full stop. Plain hyphens only in compound words.
- Numbers: only numbers that appear in cv-canonical.md. Never invent metrics.
- Avoid: "passionate", "results-driven", "synergy", "rockstar", "open to work", "available for hire", "8+ years" style counts unless positioning.md allows them.
- Prefer: product names, stack names, what changed for users or the team.
- Tone: confident, calm, specific. No exclamation marks.
